# Brains, Bodies, and Beats: Making Sense of (Neuro)physiological Synchrony in Collective Music Experiences

Slides and materials for a 90-minute workshop at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics (Frankfurt, 2026), by Wim Pouw and Travis J. Wiltshire (Tilburg University).

The workshop asks what counts as *genuine* interpersonal (neuro)physiological synchrony in shared music experiences, beyond two people being exposed to the same stimulus. It combines a short primer, a shared listening experience, two breakout groups (conceptualization and operationalization), and a joint integration discussion.

**Live slides: https://travisjwiltshire.github.io/MPI.EA.synchrony.workshop.2026/**

## Contents

- `MPI_workshop_2026.qmd`: Quarto (reveal.js) source of the slides
- `index.html`: rendered slides, served via GitHub Pages
- `workshop.scss`: slide theme
- `img/`: images used in the slides, including the two conceptual figures (PNG and editable SVG)
- `figures/interbrain_synchrony_figures.pptx`: PowerPoint source of the two conceptual figures

To rebuild the slides, run `quarto render MPI_workshop_2026.qmd`.
